How Mawjood works, and what we are not.

Where stock data comes from

Three sources of truth behind every result.

  1. 1

    Pharmacies update

    Licensed pharmacies mark items in stock.

  2. 2

    We timestamp

    Every result shows when it was last updated.

  3. 3

    Freshness rules

    Old data is shown as Unconfirmed, never as in stock.

What we are

  • YesA search tool for medicine availability
  • YesA way to send a reservation request
  • YesA free service for people in Jordan

What we are not

  • NoA pharmacy: only licensed pharmacists dispense
  • NoA shop: there is no checkout or payment
  • NoA source of medical advice

Your privacy

We keep no health records. Your name and phone are only sent to the pharmacy you choose. Demand insights are aggregated and anonymous.
