OUR PURPOSE

Make the next step easier.

When someone needs a medicine, the search can involve several calls, visits, and unanswered questions. Mawjood is designed to make that first step more straightforward by bringing pharmacy information and medicine listings into one easy-to-use experience.

We want to help people make more informed decisions about where to ask, while helping pharmacies share useful information with their communities.

OUR COMMITMENT

Helpful pharmacy information, with people first.

Mawjood is intended to make discovery and communication easier—not to replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

  • ✓Clarity over confusionWe aim to present pharmacy and medicine information in a straightforward way.
  • ✓Connection, not guessworkContact a pharmacy to confirm current stock, prices, and opening hours.
  • ✓Respect for professional careFor diagnosis, treatment, or medicine safety questions, speak with a pharmacist or clinician.

A NOTE ON AVAILABILITY

Listings are a starting point—not a guarantee.

Medicine stock, prices, and pharmacy hours can change. Always confirm details directly with the pharmacy before travelling or making a decision. Mawjood does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.
